Reality checks

行前須知
🌦️Late-November weather

Taipei 18–22 °C with rain chances · lake 15–20 °C · Wuling summit 0–5 °C · Kaohsiung 26 °C and sunny. Pack layers, gloves, rain shells, swimwear for the tubs.

🚫Why west, not east

Taroko's famous trails remain closed in 2026, and the east-side full KOM has no scheduled single-day group rides in November — only private vans. The west climb is the more beautiful kilometre anyway.

🚶Alishan is for legs, not pedals

No bike rentals up there — it's shuttle, walkways and sunrise. Your quads will file a thank-you note.

🚉No car, ever

HSR + TRA + 台灣好行 tourist buses + MRT + the odd taxi cover every leg. Group rides include their own support van.
